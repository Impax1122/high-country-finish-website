## Findings

### Fixed in the repo

1. **Live site linked to a different URL than the canonical.** Netlify "pretty URLs" post-processing
   rewrote every internal link to `/page` while canonicals, sitemap, Open Graph URLs and breadcrumbs
   used `/page.html`; both forms returned 200. Fixed with `pretty_urls = false` in `netlify.toml`.
   If you ever want clean URLs instead: change the canonical/`og:url`/`@id`/breadcrumb/sitemap values
   in one pass, drop `.html` from internal links, and add a `_redirects` file with a 301 per page.
2. **Thin blog content.** The five March posts were 320–420 words with no images. All five are now
   900–1,100 words with a portfolio photo, a table, and cross-links, at the same URLs, with
   `dateModified` and a visible "Updated" date. Six new posts (1,000–1,600 words) were added.
3. **Blog reachable only from nav and footer.** The homepage now has a "From the Blog" strip with the
   three commercial posts above the quote form.
4. **No privacy policy.** `privacy.html` describes exactly what the site does today (Formspree form,
   Netlify logs, Google Fonts, no cookies, no analytics). Linked from the footer on every page.
   Have an attorney read it before you rely on it, and update it when analytics or payments are added.
5. **Homepage `meta keywords`** (ignored by Google, listed client names) removed.
6. **Sitemap** now includes the blog figures as image entries; **`feed.xml`** (RSS 2.0) is generated from
   the posts' `BlogPosting` schema and linked from the blog pages.
7. **Quote forms** (homepage and quote page) now offer "ADA / Code-Required Signage" and
   "Install-Only Work (Sign Shops & Contractors)", matching the new content and the packages plan.

### Still open — owner tasks

1. **Google Business Profile and reviews.** Unchanged since launch and still the biggest lever for
   "sign installation Denver" style searches. Create and verify the profile, upload the portfolio
   photos, ask the last ten commercial clients for reviews, then add the profile URL (and Instagram /
   LinkedIn when they exist) as `sameAs` on the `LocalBusiness` schema in `index.html`.
2. **Analytics.** Nothing is measured. Plausible or Fathom need no cookie banner; GA4 is free.
   After choosing one: add its script tag, extend `script-src` and `connect-src` in the
   `Content-Security-Policy` in `netlify.toml`, update `privacy.html`, and treat the quote form's
   success state (`#form-success` becoming visible) as the conversion.
3. **Search Console.** Resubmit `sitemap.xml` and request indexing for the six new posts and the
   five updated ones. Watch the Pages report for the extensionless URLs dropping out.
4. **Distribute the code posts.** The fire-door and ADA articles are written to earn links from
   property managers, general contractors and facility groups. Share them on LinkedIn and send them
   to two or three property management contacts.
5. **City pages.** Three to five real pages (Arvada, Lakewood/Golden, Boulder, Aurora, Littleton/
   Castle Rock) with unique copy and local project photos. Not a template.
6. **Business facts the site cannot supply.** `openingHoursSpecification` for the schema, social
   profile links, and whether to credit the lead installer as a named author on the code posts.
7. **Optional polish.** Self-host the two Google Fonts (saves 100–200 ms first paint); refresh the
   homepage hero photo seasonally; add the "Packages" page from `ECOMMERCE_ROADMAP.md` when ready.
